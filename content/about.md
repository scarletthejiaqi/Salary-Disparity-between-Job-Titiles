## About

**U.S. Pay & Hiring Trends** tracks offered salaries for 52 professional roles from 2019 to now, by seniority, employer and location, together with market-wide hiring demand.

### Project history

This is the **updated version (v2)** of an earlier coursework project, *Salary Disparity between Job Titles*, which inspired it.

- **Version 1 (coursework).** A Shiny app built on a static Kaggle dataset (*Salary by Job Title and Country*, about 6,700 records). It plotted salary against years of experience by job title, country and education level. The dataset had no time dimension, so it could not show trends.
- **Version 2 (this app).** It keeps the original question of how pay differs between job titles and seniority levels. It now answers it with about 4 million real, government-published job offers from 2019 to now, a role and seniority classification, inflation adjustment, and hiring-demand data. All of it is rebuilt by a reproducible, tested pipeline.

The version 1 code is preserved in the project's Git history.

**Use it to**

- benchmark an offer against what employers file for the same role, level and state;
- see whether pay for your role is keeping up with inflation;
- spot which roles and seniority levels employers are hiring for more, or less;
- find the employers that sponsor the most positions in your field.
